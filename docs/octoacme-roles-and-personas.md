# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, create test strategies, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to validate that solutions meet customer needs and organizational quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned with project scope and milestones
- Define acceptance criteria validation approach (unit, integration, e2e, manual testing)
- Execute and coordinate testing activities across team members
- Identify and triage defects, track quality metrics
- Participate in release readiness reviews and sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure zero critical bugs reach production
- Reduce time to detect and fix quality issues
- Build confidence in release readiness through thorough validation

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and test plan reviews during planning
- Defect triage updates in daily standups and sprint reviews
- Release readiness and quality gate sign-off updates

---

## Stakeholders/Sponsors

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Stakeholders provide business context, approve resource allocation, and make key business decisions. They represent customer needs and business priorities, ensuring projects align with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and success metrics
- Provide business context and priority guidance
- Participate in decision gates (go/no-go decisions)
- Receive and review milestone updates and risk communications
- Support team escalations and remove organizational blockers

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Minimize risk to business outcomes
- Enable clear, timely executive visibility into project health

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and monthly stakeholder updates
- Decision logs and go/no-go gate approvals
- Escalation check-ins for business-impacting issues

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define system design, evaluate technical trade-offs, and identify risks. They work closely with developers and stakeholders to ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical approach and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Guide developers on design patterns and best practices
- Participate in design reviews and code reviews for architectural concerns
- Support estimation and capacity planning with technical insight

### Goals
- Deliver architecturally sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and long-term risk
- Enable team to build with clarity and confidence

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review sessions during planning and major scope changes
- PR and design review feedback on technical trade-offs
- Weekly syncs with PM and engineering leads on technical risk

---

## Communications/Scrum Leads

### Role Summary
Scrum/Communications Leads facilitate team ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives) and ensure clear, timely communication across the team and stakeholders. They remove process blockers and keep projects flowing smoothly.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Maintain project board and ensure backlog is current and visible
- Track action items from ceremonies and follow up on completion
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communications
- Support Project Manager with schedule and dependency coordination

### Goals
- Keep team synchronized and focused on priorities
- Ensure ceremonies add value and respect team time
- Reduce unplanned context-switching and miscommunication

### Typical Communication
- Daily facilitation notes and action item follow-up
- Sprint planning, review, and retrospective summaries
- Cross-team status broadcasts and blocker escalations to PM

---

## Interaction Patterns
- **Developers + QA/Testing Lead:** Partner on test planning during sprint planning; review test results during sprint review.
- **Product Manager + Stakeholder/Sponsor:** Align on success metrics and priorities during initiation and planning gates.
- **Technical Architect + Developers:** Collaborate on design during planning; architect reviews technical decisions in PRs.
- **Communications/Scrum Lead + All Roles:** Facilitates ceremonies and project communication; escalates blockers to the Project Manager.
- **Project Manager + Technical Architect/QA Lead:** Coordinate risk mitigation and integration points in weekly sync.
